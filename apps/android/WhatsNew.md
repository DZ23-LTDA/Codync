## 2.34.1
### zh-Hant
- 介面現在有巴西葡萄牙文（Português (Brasil)），可在設定裡選擇。

### en-US
- The app is now available in Brazilian Portuguese, chosen in Settings.
- Tapping the background of a conversation dismisses the keyboard.
- The conversation list keeps the newest message at the bottom.
