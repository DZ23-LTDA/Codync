## 2.29.0

### zh-Hant
- 評分邀請只會在你使用一段時間、看完 Bot 的成果並回到列表後出現，不會在開啟 App 時打擾你。也可以隨時從設定前往 App Store 評分。
- 設定裡每台電腦多了網路按鈕：看你的電腦網路裡有哪些電腦、是否在線、各自提供哪些 Bot，以及雲端助理是否開啟。網路的建立與邀請要在電腦上的 Codync 進行。

### en-US
- Rating invitations wait until you have used Codync for a while, viewed a bot's result, and returned to the list. You can also rate Codync anytime from Settings.
- A network button on each computer in Settings shows the computers in your network, whether each one answers, the bots it offers, and whether cloud assistants are on. You set up and invite computers in Codync on a computer.
